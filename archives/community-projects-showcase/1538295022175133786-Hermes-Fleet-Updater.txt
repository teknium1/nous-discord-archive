    One safe command to update Nous Research Hermes on every machine you run it on — VPS, home server, laptop — over your own network. Backs up, stops what's holding the binary, updates, restarts, verifies, and never leaves an instance down.
    
    The problem
    You run Hermes (hermes-agent) on several machines — a VPS, a home box/NAS, a laptop — usually reachable over Tailscale. Keeping them updated is painful:
    
    hermes update is per-machine and manual — there's no "update all my instances".
    On Windows/macOS the update fails while a serve/gateway holds the binary (Another hermes.exe is running) — so you must stop services first, in the right order, then restart them.
    Updates can migrate the config and drop settings (e.g. a serve's auth block) → the service crash-loops and the instance is silently down.
    You forget a box, or leave one half-updated.
    Result: fragmented versions, surprise outages, and dread around updating.
