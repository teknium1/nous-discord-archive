    Guys, I’ve been working on a LIVE activity visual extension HUD to Hermes desk top app. The Hermes Sigil Live Activity Overlay is a transparent Windows desktop extension that turns Hermes Desktop activity into a live, animated visual experience. As Hermes thinks, searches, browses, executes tools, creates content, monitors tasks, or responds, the sigil reacts in real time through animated nodes, mechanical motion, workflow paths, status effects, and completion signals.
    
    A privacy-preserving local bridge connects directly to Hermes without exposing prompts, responses, files, credentials, or other sensitive content. The overlay also includes customizable interaction controls, click-through functionality, hotkeys, and a built-in Activity Inspector for monitoring and troubleshooting Hermes activity.
    
    In short, it gives Hermes a live visual presence on your desktop, allowing you to see what your AI agent is doing without needing to constantly watch the Hermes application itself. The graphics hardly even take up 5% of my GPU so it could also be ran on a laptop
