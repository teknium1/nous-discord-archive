    Hey everyone 👋 Been quietly building something called AgentOnRails.
    
    It’s fully open source + local-first. Basically a layer that sits between your agent and the internet and gives it financial guardrails — budgets, per-transaction limits, approvals — while also working toward identity + privacy controls, all without sending your agent’s activity through some hosted middleman.
    
    Hermes has basically been our partner in crime 😂 throughout the process so of course there’s a native Hermes integration in the repo.
    
    Still early, so I’d genuinely love for some of you to try it, break it, build on it, or tell me what’s missing. Would love feedback from people actually building/using agents. Curious what you guys think.
