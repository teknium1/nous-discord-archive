    Added a topbar app for Mac and a minimal app for Windows. Very minimal, click to expand the menu. 
    
    I hated using other agentic memory, because it would inevitably unlink, and then I would just keep using the agent without knowing it was broken, wasting tons of time. Now I can click the topbar, see everything's running, toggle on options such as whether or not to save new memories, and view the last 5 memories saved at a quick glance. It also makes it easy to lock and unlock the encrypted memory.
