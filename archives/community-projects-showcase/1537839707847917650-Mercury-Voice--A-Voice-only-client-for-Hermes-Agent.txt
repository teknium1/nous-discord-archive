    First things first: I am not a developer, just a sysadmin cosplaying as one with a Fable trenchcoat.
    
    I loved the ChatGPT Voice feature, but I don't have much setup in ChatGPT as far as plugins and what not. So I started working with Claude Code and ChatGPT to develop an iOS / MacOS app that brings a lot of the ChatGPT Voice functionality to Hermes. 
    
    I don't have a full Apple Developer account, so I can't release this to the app store, and all testing has been done by sideloading the app via Xcode.
    
    That being said, it's been pretty stable and usable on my drive to / from work the past couple of days, and the people I have mentioned this to wanted the same app. So I decided to post the source to my Github, with no promises that it will work for you or wont start WW3. 
    
    I'd welcome any feedback, PR's, issue submission, etc, to make this a better client. I am tempted at this point to pay the $100 to release this to the app store, but really have no reason to unless more people want this, and some actual developers take a look at the code first.
    
    Anyways, here's the github: https://github.com/angel12/mercury-voice
