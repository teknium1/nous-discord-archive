    Built something with Hermes Agent I wanted to share: Molt, an MCP server that lets an agent spend money under hard limits. You sign a budget once with a passkey; every purchase mints a single-use card scoped to one store and one exact cart. Unknown merchant → the purchase holds until a passkey tap on my phone; Hermes waits and continues on its own. All test mode (Stripe sandbox + testnet USDC), Apache 2.0. 5-min demo with Hermes doing the shopping: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AZ_xv7lazl0&t=4s repo: github.com/meyerdav24/molt. Happy for feedback and holes poked.
