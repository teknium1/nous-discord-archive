    Yes — exactly, with one important distinction: the phone is the control surface, while the actual dev environment runs on your connected machine or server.
    From Hermes Go, you can ask Hermes to scaffold a project, edit files, run terminal commands, install dependencies, start background processes, inspect output, review Git diffs, and keep iterating — all from your phone.
    As long as the host has the repo and toolchain, stays online, and you have the required permissions, you can build remotely over LAN, Tailscale, or Hermes Cloud. It’s not a full IDE running on the phone; it’s more like a mobile control center for your remote development environment.
