    Pick a problem and work through a solution end to end. Your agent will turn that into a skill and the next time you want to solve that same problem, your agent will do it faster. No minimum plugins or MCPs needed unless you have a particular desire to get some specific extended capabilities.
    
    Take one thing from your day-to-day and have hermes run it end to end with your guidance.
    
    If you have a list of things to work on (eg 5 campaigns to analyse) give hermes the info and tell hermes what you want done with it once you're comfortable with the outcome tell hermes to check some location (eg email) everyday so it can pull that list for you and then you get one end to end workflow completed per day
