    Most code review tools check for style violations and linting. Hermaguard tries to break your code.
    
    https://github.com/Sahil-SS9/hermaguard
    
        Three parallel subagents attack your diff from different angles: an edge case hunter that exhaustively traces every branching path in the change, an adversarial reviewer with a deliberately cynical persona ("your job is to break confidence, not validate"), and a blast radius mapper that traces every caller and callee to catch downstream breakage.
    
        Then a consolidator merges, de-duplicates, and triages everything into a risk-tiered report (CRITICAL/HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW).
    
        It's read-only — finds problems, doesn't touch your code. Opt-in only, no auto-trigger on commit. Run /hermaguard when you want to know what you missed.
    
        Built by synthesising 8 existing implementations: Trail of Bits differential review, BMAD's edge case hunter and adversarial reviewer, dementev-dev's Claude-Codex review loop, Anthropic's security review action, and the adversarial prompt pattern from r/ClaudeAI.
    
        PR is open: #42171
    
        If you write a skill that pairs with this (fix orchestrator, auto-test generator for findings, CI integration), open a PR. Bug reports in the review process appreciated. Complaints about the adversarial agent's attitude are also valid — it's designed to be a bit of a bastard.

    Quick update to HermaGuard — nothing major, just a UX fix that's been bugging me.
    
    Previously when you invoked the skill, it would go completely silent for a few minutes while the parallel agents did their analysis. No indication anything was running, no ETA — just a blank screen. Easy to assume your session had hung.

    Hermaguard v2.1 is out 🛡️ — my adversarial code-review skill just got its accountability layer. What's new:
    
    The review can't lie anymore:
    - Coverage checklist — every report accounts for all 32 bug classes (MITRE CWE Top 25 + races, async gaps, partial writes). No silent skips; gaps fail the check.
    - Verified findings — agents write a PoC, a sandbox runner executes it, and the verdict comes from real execution output, never the model just claiming a bug exists. Race conditions and async bugs must carry proof before they ship in a report.
    - Honest scoring — benchmark corpus grew 5 → 25 bugs with deterministic class-matching. No fuzzy keyword scores.
    - It learns — dismiss a false positive 3× and it becomes a suppression rule applied automatically on future runs.
    
    Plus a L0–L6 harm grader, prompt-injection sanitizer for the code the agents read, pip install . with 9 CLI tools, CI, and 130 tests.
    
    Works with Hermes, Claude Code, Codex, or any harness with subagents:
    https://github.com/Sahil-SS9/hermaguard
    
    Feedback welcome — especially false-positive reports, they literally make it smarter.
