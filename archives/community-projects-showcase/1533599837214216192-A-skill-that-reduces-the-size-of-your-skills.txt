    As weird as it sounds, frontier models often perform worse when a skill over-specifies and adds too much hand-holding. That's the opposite of older models, which often benefited from detailed guidance.
    
    Boris Cherny (creator of Claude Code) has said as much directly: stop over-specifying and "let the model cook." Simpler skills let frontier models do the heavy lifting.
    
    I built **skillet** to make that cleanup easy. It's a tiny skill that audits your existing skills and strips away unnecessary over-specification.
    
    GitHub: https://github.com/fayzan123/skillet
    
    Open to suggestions or feedback on use cases.
