    🏛️ Hall of Wisdom — Development Update
    
    A lot of work has recently gone into making Hall of Wisdom simpler and easier to use.
    
    🧠 Wisdom Gateway improved
    You can now manage much more of the workflow directly from the Gateway.
    
    A task can move through:
    
    Ask → Plan → Approve → Choose Agent → Prepare → Start → Monitor → Result
    
    You can also see the current step and live progress while the agents are working.
    
    👀 New “Needs Your Attention” area
    Hall now collects important things that require your input in one place, such as:
    
    Plans waiting for approval
    Failed or blocked tasks
    Agent runs that need intervention
    
    So instead of searching around the application, Hall tells you what needs your attention.
    
    🧭 Simpler UI and navigation
    The interface has been cleaned up and grouped into just a few main areas:
    
    Home • Work • Attention • Team • Settings
    
    Advanced information is still available, but technical details are hidden unless you actually need them.
    
    💬 Discuss work directly
    Tasks and plans can now open their own discussion area, making it easier to talk about a task without losing the context.
    
    ✅ Better work results
    After a run finishes, Hall now shows a simple result summary — how many steps completed, what failed, and what may need review.
    
    ⚡ In simple terms
    
    Hall of Wisdom is moving closer to the idea of:
    
    You tell Hall what you want → Hall creates a plan → agents do the work → Hall keeps you updated.
    
    The goal is to make multi-agent workflows usable even for people who don't want to deal with complicated agent configuration.
    
    🧪 The project is still under active development.
    
    Linux testing, bug reports, UI feedback, feature ideas, and contributions are very welcome!
    
    More updates soon 🚀
