    If you’re like me, out of sight = out of mind. Random ideas, intrusive thoughts, tangents that aren't enough to start their own sessions yet - that die if I don’t jot them down. Notes, Keep, texts to myself, paper pad on the desk - then I have five places to remember to follow back on that inevitably get forgotten or fall through the cracks...
    
    Hermes is *always* open on my desktop. 
    So I built sticky notes into it - 'stickies'! Like post-its you would put on the side of your screen, but living inside Hermes.
    
    Break out = stand alone sticky note in Hermes window. Drag them together = pile. Stack all = clean UI, still remember that random thought.
    
    Global, profile-scoped storage. Pure Hermes Desktop plugin SDK, no app fork.
    
    Already shared to X, but thought I'd share here too as a way of introducing myself in the Discord 😋 🤘
